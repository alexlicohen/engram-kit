# glossary

Acronyms and recurring terms used across the store. One line each; link the first appearance in a
note here rather than re-defining it.

- **MOC** — "map of content": a generated `_index.md` listing a domain's (or area's) notes.
- **distilled vs verbatim** — whether a source was compressed into atomic notes or preserved untouched
  (`provenance.method`).
- **provenance ref** — the stable source pointer in every note's frontmatter (`granola:<id>`,
  `gmail:thread:<id>`, `research:<slug>`, …); the dedup and audit keystone.
- **wave** — one batch of source items handed to a distiller under a one-shot contract.
